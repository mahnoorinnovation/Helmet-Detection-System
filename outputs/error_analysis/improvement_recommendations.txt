
MODEL IMPROVEMENT RECOMMENDATIONS

1. More Diverse Training Data:
Add more annotated images containing different camera angles,
lighting conditions, crowded scenes, and motorcycle backgrounds.

2. Annotation Quality Improvement:
Carefully review bounding boxes around people and helmets.
Correct inaccurate or inconsistent annotations to reduce confusion
between motorcycles and helmet-wearing persons.

3. Data Augmentation and Model Tuning:
Use suitable augmentation for blurry, small-object, and low-light
images. Experiment with confidence thresholds and other inference
settings to reduce false detections.
